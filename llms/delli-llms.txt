# DELLI

> DELLI is a UK online marketplace for independent food and drink makers, founded in 2022 by Depop founder Simon Beckerman. Often called "the Depop for food," it curates niche and small-batch products and has evolved into an AI-powered companion for makers and retailers. Backed by Balderton Capital and HV Capital. The storefront runs on Shopify; there is no public first-party developer API at this time.
